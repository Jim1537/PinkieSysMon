# Development Approach & AI Disclosure

## 100% Vibe-Coded

I have experience developing various kinds of application software. Nevertheless, **Pinkie's System Monitor is 100% the result of vibe coding**.

For this project, I deliberately used AI-assisted coding to turn my ideas, requirements, and feedback into an application, rather than treating it as an exercise in writing everything by hand.

## Why This Approach?

The motivation was simple: **I had bought a new PC and wanted a fun system monitor for it**.

What I wanted was a useful, good-looking result—not to spend months or years developing what is, in essence, a fairly straightforward utility. Vibe coding was a conscious choice to focus my time on the result instead of the mechanics of writing every part of the program from scratch.

This disclosure is here for transparency, not as an excuse for defects or a claim that the software is experimental. The project's release status, supported display, and limits of real-world testing are documented separately in [Compatibility & Testing](../compatibility.md).

If you try the application or review its code, feedback and [GitHub issues](https://github.com/Jim1537/PinkieSysMon/issues) are welcome.
